## Videos that do well have a reason

A video that blows up isn't luck — it's structure: a hook in the first 0–15 seconds, an arc that holds tension, an ending that pulls you to the next one. Story mode's **1 Research step** pulls exactly that structure out of reference videos so you can transplant it into your own story.

![Story mode Research step — reference video structure analysis, viral score, fact-check](/images/blog/story/story-benchmark-hero-en.png)

Research finds reference videos by keyword, **analyzes their structure (hook, arc, ending)**, and shows a **viral score** for the standouts. You start from data about what actually works, not a gut feeling.

## What the Research step does

- **Keyword search** — finds reference YouTube videos by the keywords of your topic. (You can even confirm research with keywords alone, before a title exists.)
- **Structure analysis** — breaks down each video's skeleton: the hook, the 3-act arc, twists, the cliffhanger ending.
- **Viral score** — prioritizes which videos to benchmark, based on views and engagement.
- **Fact-check** — cross-checks the core claims you'll build the story on, preventing a script whose facts don't hold up.

## Research → Synopsis → Script

Research is the pipeline's **first run step (after Setup, before Synopsis)**. The structure analysis and fact-checked claims you confirm here become the raw material for **2 Synopsis**, which in turn feeds **3 Script**.

![Story mode stepper — the Research step active](/images/blog/story/story-benchmark-pipeline-en.png)

So the "grammar of videos that work" is baked into the synopsis and script from the very start. Instead of writing a script blind and then wondering why nobody watched, you build the story **on top of a proven structure**.

> 💡 Research and Synopsis are **gate tabs**, not run steps. They activate on projects that start from a title with a new topic, and lock on projects where you've already pasted a script.

## From there, business as usual

Once Research sets the direction, the rest matches the [title-only flow](/en/blog/story-mode-title-to-video/) — Synopsis → Script → Scene split → Audio → Prompts, then **export to CapCut, Premiere, or Vrew in one click**.

## In short

Benchmarking isn't copying — it's **learning the structure**. Story mode's Research step analyzes a reference video's hook, arc, and ending and verifies its claims, then weaves that grammar naturally into your synopsis and script.
