Snowbeat Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026 · 한국어

Summary

Snowbeat has no accounts. There is no sign-in, no registration and no email collection. We do not ask for your name, phone number, address or payment details, and your settings and records stay on your device. Only two things ever leave it: multiplayer position data and advertising.

1. Stored on your device only (never transmitted)

Kept in local storage and removed when you uninstall the app.

  • Settings: music and SFX volume, camera distance, time of day, graphics, language, inverted controls
  • Personal records: distance, date and wipeout coordinates of your three longest runs
  • Display name: the nickname used in multiplayer (optional — the game works without one)
  • Rhythm settings: selected song, tap latency calibration
  • Imported songs: MIDI files you chose yourself

2. Transmitted during multiplayer

When you start a run, the game exchanges live positions so that other riders appear on screen.

  • Sent to: wss://flowline.games.bu.app — a server operated by Flowline, the project this app is derived from. It is a third-party server not operated by Touchizen, used with the original author's permission.
  • What is sent: display name, colour index, in-game coordinates (x, y, z), heading, posture, speed and distance travelled, refreshed about every 0.075 seconds.
  • What is NOT sent: email, phone number, contacts, photos, real-world location (GPS), device identifiers, advertising ID, payment details.
  • Retention: this data exists to draw other riders in real time. Touchizen does not store it or use it for any other purpose.

You choose your display name. Please do not use your real name or anything identifying — other players can see it.

3. Advertising

This app shows Google AdMob ads. After a wipeout it offers a rewarded ad to continue your run, and declining costs you nothing in the game.

  • Google may collect the advertising ID, device information and IP address to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's own policies. Touchizen does not receive that data.
  • See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. Children

This app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Deletion

Everything stored on the device is removed when you uninstall the app. There is no account, so there is nothing to close.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top changes with it.
